---
title: "$numberSeparator"
---

Separates a number in thousands

## Usage

```cc
$numberSeparator[number;separator (optional)]
```

### Example:
<DiscordMessages>
<DiscordMessageUser>
!!exec Your number is $numberSeparator[3352311]<br /><br />
</DiscordMessageUser>
<DiscordMessageBot>
Your number is 3,352,311
</DiscordMessageBot>
</DiscordMessages>
