---
title: "$stringStartsWith"
---

Determines whether given message starts by another message or not

## Usage

```cc
$stringStartsWith[message;text]
```

### Example:
<DiscordMessages>
<DiscordMessageUser>
!!exec $stringStartsWith[Hello World;Hello]<br /><br />
</DiscordMessageUser>
<DiscordMessageBot>
true<br /><br />
</DiscordMessageBot>
</DiscordMessages>

### Example:
<DiscordMessages>
<DiscordMessageUser>
!!exec $stringStartsWith[Hello World;Hate]<br /><br />
</DiscordMessageUser>
<DiscordMessageBot>
false
</DiscordMessageBot>
</DiscordMessages>
