---
title: "$customEmoji"
---

Returns a custom emoji

## Usage

```cc
$customEmoji[name or id]
```

### Example (Using Custom Emoji Name):
<DiscordMessages>
<DiscordMessageUser>
!!exec $customEmoji[yes]<br /><br />
</DiscordMessageUser>
<DiscordMessageBot>
<DiscordEmbed slot="embeds" image="https://i.imgur.com/E174Ey0.png"><br />
</DiscordEmbed>
</DiscordMessageBot>
</DiscordMessages>

### Example (Using Custom Emoji ID):
<DiscordMessages>
<DiscordMessageUser>
!!exec $customEmoji[833252579873259521]<br /><br />
</DiscordMessageUser>
<DiscordMessageBot>
<DiscordEmbed slot="embeds" image="https://i.imgur.com/7t3gZNn.png"><br />
</DiscordEmbed>
</DiscordMessageBot>
</DiscordMessages>
