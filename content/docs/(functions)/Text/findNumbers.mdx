---
title: "$findNumbers"
---

Find numbers from inside a text.

## Usage

```cc
$findNumbers[text;separator]
```

### Example:
<DiscordMessages>
<DiscordMessageUser>
!!exec $findNumbers[My age is 99 years old, and located 15 miles away from nearest station.;, ]<br /><br />
</DiscordMessageUser>
<DiscordMessageBot>
99, 15
</DiscordMessageBot>
</DiscordMessages>
