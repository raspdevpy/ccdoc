---
title: "$findNumbers"
---

Find numbers from inside a text.

## Usage

```cc
$findNumbers[text;separator]
```

### Example:

<Discord>
    <Message>
        !!exec $findNumbers[My age is 99 years old, and located 15 miles away
        from nearest station.;, ]
    </Message>
    <Message bot>99, 15</Message>
</Discord>
