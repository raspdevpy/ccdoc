---
title: "$regexReplace"
---

Uses a regular expression to replace matching queries

## Usage

```cc
$regexReplace[text;regex;flags;new text]
```

### Example 1:

<Discord>
    <Message>
        !!exec $regexReplace[My age is 900 years old.;\d+;g;[SECRET AGE]]
    </Message>
    <Message bot>My age is [SECRET AGE] years old.</Message>
</Discord>
