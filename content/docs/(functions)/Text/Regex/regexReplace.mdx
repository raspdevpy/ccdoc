---
title: "$regexReplace"
---

Uses a regular expression to replace matching queries

## Usage

```cc
$regexReplace[text;regex;flags;new text]
```

### Example 1:
<DiscordMessages>
<DiscordMessageUser>
!!exec $regexReplace[My age is 900 years old.;\d+;g;[SECRET AGE]]<br /><br />
</DiscordMessageUser>
<DiscordMessageBot>
My age is [SECRET AGE] years old.
</DiscordMessageBot>
</DiscordMessages>
