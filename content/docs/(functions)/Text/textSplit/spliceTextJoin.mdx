---
title: "$spliceTextJoin"
---

Splits a text with `separator1`, then joins with it `separator2` every `x` times, and then joins with `separator3` every `x-1` times.

## Usage

```cc
$spliceTextJoin[text;separator1;separator2;separator3;every]
```
