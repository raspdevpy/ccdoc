---
title: "$filterMessage"
---

Removes letters or numbers from given text

## Usage

```cc
$filterMessage[message;letterOrSymbols]
```

### Example:
<DiscordMessages>
<DiscordMessageUser>
!!exec $filterMessage[Hello World;lo]<br /><br />
</DiscordMessageUser>
<DiscordMessageBot>
He Wrd
</DiscordMessageBot>
</DiscordMessages>
