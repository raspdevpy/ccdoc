---
title: "$numToWord"
---

convert a number to their verbal equivalents i.\
e 5 \> five, maximum number would be "nine hundred ninety-nine nonillion"

## Usage

```cc
$numToWord[Number]
```

### Example:

<Discord>
    <Message>!!exec $numToWord[1001]</Message>
    <Message bot>one thousand one</Message>
</Discord>
