---
title: "$numToWord"
---

Convert a number to their verbal equivalents (e.g. 5 -\> five). The numbers cap out at "nine hundred ninety-nine nonillion" (999999999999999999999999999999999) without throwing an error.

## Usage

```cc
$numToWord[Number]
```
