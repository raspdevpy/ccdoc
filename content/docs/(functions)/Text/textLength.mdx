---
title: "$textLength"
---

Counts character of a text, or the user's message.

## Usage

```cc
$textLength or $textLength[text]
```

### Example:
<DiscordMessages>
<DiscordMessageUser>
!!exec $textLength[Mido]<br /><br />
</DiscordMessageUser>
<DiscordMessageBot>
4<br /><br />
</DiscordMessageBot>
</DiscordMessages>

### Example:
<DiscordMessages>
<DiscordMessageUser>
!!exec $textLength[Hello]<br /><br />
</DiscordMessageUser>
<DiscordMessageBot>
6
</DiscordMessageBot>
</DiscordMessages>
