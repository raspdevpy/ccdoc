---
title: "$textTrim"
---

Removes useless spaces from given text.

## Usage

```cc
$textTrim[text]
```

### Example:
<DiscordMessages>
<DiscordMessageUser>
!!exec $textTrim[             My name is Mido             ]<br /><br />
</DiscordMessageUser>
<DiscordMessageBot>
My name is Mido
</DiscordMessageBot>
</DiscordMessages>
