---
title: "$floor"
---

return the largest integer less than or equal to a given number

## Usage

```cc
$floor[Number]
```

### Example:
<DiscordMessages>
<DiscordMessageUser>
!!exec $floor[2.4]<br /><br />
</DiscordMessageUser>

2<br /><br />
</DiscordMessageBot>
