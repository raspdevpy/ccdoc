---
title: "$mathMax"
---

Return the largest number from a list of numbers

## Usage

```cc
$mathMax[Number 1;Number 2;Number 3;...]
```

### Example:
<DiscordMessages>
<DiscordMessageUser>
!!exec $mathMax[100;50;150]<br /><br />
</DiscordMessageUser>
<DiscordMessageBot>
150
</DiscordMessageBot>
</DiscordMessages>
