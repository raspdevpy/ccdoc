---
title: "$abbreviate"
---

this function abbreviates large numbers
Abbreviation to:
* k - thousands
* m - millions
* b - billions
* t - trillions

#### Usage: `$abbreviate[number]`
<br/>
<DiscordMessages>
<DiscordMessageUser>
!!exec $abbreviate[6000]
</DiscordMessageUser>
<DiscordMessageBot>
6k
</DiscordMessageBot>
</DiscordMessages>
