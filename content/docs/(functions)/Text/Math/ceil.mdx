---
title: "$ceil"
---

rounds a number up to the next largest integer

## Usage

```cc
$ceil[Number]
```

### Example:
<DiscordMessages>
<DiscordMessageUser>
!!exec $ceil[1.3]<br /><br />
</DiscordMessageUser>
<DiscordMessageBot>
2<br /><br />
</DiscordMessageBot>
</DiscordMessages>
