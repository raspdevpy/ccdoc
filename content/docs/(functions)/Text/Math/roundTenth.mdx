---
title: "$roundTenth"
---

Rounds the number to the nearest decimal specified in `toFixed`

#### Usage: `$roundTenth[number;tofixed]`
<br/>
<DiscordMessages>
<DiscordMessageUser>
!!exec $roundTenth[10.877890790;2]
</DiscordMessageUser>
<DiscordMessageBot>
10.88
</DiscordMessageBot>
</DiscordMessages>
