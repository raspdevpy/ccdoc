---
title: "$padRight"
---

Adds a filling text at the end of text, depend on the maximum length

## Usage

```cc
$padRight[Text;Max Length;Filling Text]
```

### Example:
<DiscordMessages>
<DiscordMessageUser>
!!exec $padRight[I like custom commands;25;.]<br /><br />
</DiscordMessageUser>
<DiscordMessageBot>
I like custom commands...
</DiscordMessageBot>
</DiscordMessages>
