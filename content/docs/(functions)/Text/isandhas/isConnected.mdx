---
title: "$isConnected"
---

To check whether user is connected to voice channel or not (only cached users)

## Usage

```cc
$isConnected[User ID]
```

### Example:
<DiscordMessages>
<DiscordMessageUser>
!!exec Is $username Connected To Channel?: $isConnected<br /><br />
</DiscordMessageUser>
<DiscordMessageBot>
Is Mido Connected To Channel?: true
</DiscordMessageBot>
</DiscordMessages>
