---
title: "$textSlice"
---

Returns \<text\> after given position or text in between X and Y

## Usage

```cc
$textSlice[text;x;y (optional)]
```

### Example:
<DiscordMessages>
<DiscordMessageUser>
!!exec $textSlice[Hello world;0;5]<br /><br />
</DiscordMessageUser>
<DiscordMessageBot>
Hello
</DiscordMessageBot>
</DiscordMessages>
