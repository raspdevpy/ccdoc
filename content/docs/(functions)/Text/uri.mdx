---
title: "$uri"
---

Decodes or Encodes a url Example when you encode a url 'hello world' = 'hello%20world'

## Usage

```cc
$uri[decode/encode;text]
```

### Example (Encoding):
<DiscordMessages>
<DiscordMessageUser>
!!exec $uri[encode;Hello World]<br /><br />
</DiscordMessageUser>
<DiscordMessageBot>
Hello%20World<br /><br />
</DiscordMessageBot>
</DiscordMessages>

### Example (Decoding):
<DiscordMessages>
<DiscordMessageUser>
!!exec $uri[decode;Hello%20World]<br /><br />
</DiscordMessageUser>
<DiscordMessageBot>
Hello World
</DiscordMessageBot>
</DiscordMessages>
