---
title: "$toLocaleUppercase[text]"
---

this sentence uppercases every first char of a word in a sentence

#### Usage: 
`$toLocaleUppercase[text]`

<br/>
<DiscordMessages>
<DiscordMessageUser>
!!exec $toLocaleUppercase[hello how are you?]
</DiscordMessageUser>
<DiscordMessageBot>
Hello How Are You?
</DiscordMessageBot>
</DiscordMessages>
