title: "$get"
---

retrieve variable defined by `$let`

## Usage: `$get[varname;value if not exists] or $varname`

### Example 1:
<DiscordMessages>
<DiscordMessage roleColor="#ffcc9a" author="Member">
!!exec $let[orange;10] <br/> $get[orange] or $orange
</DiscordMessage>

10 or 10
</DiscordMessage>
</DiscordMessages>
