---
title: "$rolePerms"
---

Returns the permissions a role has.

#### Usage:

`$rolePerms[roleID;separator (optional)]`

*   `roleID`: The ID of the role to check.
*   `separator`: (Optional) The separator to use when listing the permissions. Defaults to no separator.

<br/>

**Example:**
