---
title: "$botTier"
---

This command retrieves the current tier level of your bot.

The standard, free version of the bot operates at **Tier 0**.

<DiscordMessages>
<DiscordMessageUser>
!!exec $botTier
</DiscordMessageUser>
<DiscordMessageBot>
0
</DiscordMessageBot>
</DiscordMessages>

The command will then return the tier level of your bot.
